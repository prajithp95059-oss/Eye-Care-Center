Know the signs

Eye conditions we diagnose and treat

Know the early signs. Most eye problems are easier to treat when they are found early.

Cataract

Cataract is a clouding of the natural lens inside the eye. It develops slowly, so many people only notice it when everyday tasks become harder.

  • Blurred or misty vision, like looking through a dirty window
  • Glare from headlights and bright light at night
  • Colours that look faded or yellow
  • Glasses power that keeps changing

How we help: Small-incision day-care surgery replaces the cloudy lens with a clear artificial lens. Most patients go home the same day.

Book a check-up

Glaucoma

Glaucoma damages the nerve that carries images to the brain, usually because of raised pressure inside the eye. Early on it rarely causes symptoms, which is why regular checks matter.

  • Gradual loss of side vision, often unnoticed at first
  • Halos around lights or sudden eye pain (seek care at once)
  • Higher risk with family history, diabetes or age over 40
  • Detected by pressure, optic nerve and visual field tests

How we help: Prescription drops, laser treatment or surgery can slow or stop further loss. Vision already lost cannot be restored, so early detection is key.

Book a check-up

Diabetic eye disease

High blood sugar over time can damage the tiny blood vessels in the retina. It often causes no symptoms until the damage is advanced.

  • Needs a dilated retina check at least once a year
  • Sudden floaters, dark patches or blurred vision need urgent review
  • Good sugar, blood pressure and cholesterol control lowers risk
  • Digital retina photographs let us track changes over time

How we help: Laser treatment, injections or surgery are used when needed. Caught early, most vision loss from diabetes can be prevented.

Book a check-up

Dry eye and screen strain

Dry eye happens when tears are too few or evaporate too fast. Long hours on phones and computers, air conditioning and ageing all make it worse.

  • Burning, stinging or a gritty feeling in the eyes
  • Watery eyes that still feel uncomfortable
  • Tired, heavy eyes at the end of the day
  • Blurred vision that clears when you blink

How we help: Treatment may include lubricating drops, lid hygiene, screen-break habits and the 20-20-20 rule. We tailor the plan to the cause.

Book a check-up

Short-sightedness in children

Short-sightedness (myopia) often starts in school years and can progress quickly. Early correction helps children see the board and learn comfortably.

  • Squinting, rubbing eyes or frequent headaches
  • Sitting very close to the TV or holding books near
  • Struggling to read the board in class
  • Eyes that turn or one eye that seems weaker

How we help: Along with the right glasses, we advise on outdoor time, screen limits and options that can slow progression. Regular reviews keep it on track.
