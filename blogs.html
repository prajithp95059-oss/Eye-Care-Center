Daily care

Four habits for healthier eyes

Small daily choices that protect your vision for the long term.

Take screen breaks

Every 20 minutes, look at something 20 feet away for 20 seconds.

Wear UV protection

Choose sunglasses that block UV light when you are outdoors.

Do not rub your eyes

Rubbing can irritate the surface and spread infection. Use clean drops instead.

Eat well, sleep well

Leafy greens, fish and enough sleep all support healthy eyes.
